## Facts confirmed by David (25 Oct 2026)

- Base: David builds the base, or uses suitable trades, depending on the style the client wants.
- Kits: clients can choose their own pizza oven kit. Goodwin supplies and installs it. Supply and install starts from $5,500. No brand names are shown on the site.
- Build time: a pizza oven takes a minimum of one week (only a small amount of the dome can be built each day or it can collapse). Other builds vary too much to publish a timeframe.
- Permits: most of the time a permit is not needed. Goodwin does not handle permit paperwork.
- Travel: up to an hour from Camberwell for outdoor kitchen and pizza oven projects. The rest of the site still says approximately 40 minutes for inner Melbourne work.
- Photos: no finished pizza oven photos yet. The page uses two of David's in-progress oven photos.
